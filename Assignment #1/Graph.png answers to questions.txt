Questions Based on the Graph

1. What is the shape of the graph?
   The graph slopes downward from the upper left to the lower right. It then begins to plateau after approximately \(10^3\) on the rank axis.

2. What is the significance of the plot?
   The plot demonstrates Zipf’s Law: as a word’s rank increases, its frequency decreases. In other words, a small number of words appear very frequently, while many words appear only a few times.
